# Notes for App Review (paste into "App Review Information > Notes")

CupTower is a single-player climbing game. It works fully offline: no account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases,
no network access, and no data collection.

How to play: hold the left or right half of the screen to run; the character jumps automatically when it lands.
"How to play" on the title screen explains every feature; there are no hidden features or settings.

Reaching the bosses quickly: bosses normally appear every 1000 floors. To see them without climbing, tap
"BOSS FIGHTS" on the title screen and either tap "FIGHT" under any boss or "ALL IN A ROW". In boss fights, swipe up
to jump (auto jump is off only in this mode); the character fires automatically.

"Cups" shown in the Characters screen are trophies earned by playing; they cannot be bought.

All artwork, music, and sound effects are original and generated in code inside the app.
The game is available in 14 languages and follows the device language (per-app language is supported).
