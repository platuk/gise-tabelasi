# Notes for App Review (paste into "App Review Information > Notes")

CupTower is a single-player climbing game made by a small independent team. It works fully offline: no account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases, no network access, and no data collection. No third-party SDKs are included.

How to play: hold the left or right half of the screen to run; the character jumps automatically when it lands. "How to play" on the title screen explains every feature; there are no hidden features or settings.

Reaching the bosses quickly: bosses normally appear every 1000 floors. To see them without climbing, tap "BOSS FIGHTS" on the title screen and either tap "FIGHT" under any boss or "ALL IN A ROW". In boss fights, swipe up to jump (auto jump is off only in this mode); the character fires automatically. Before every side or floor attack the screen edge flashes as a warning.

What makes it original: all artwork, characters, animation, music, and sound effects are created in code inside the app (no stock or template assets, no app generator). It combines endless vertical climbing with six hand-designed boss fights (an octopus conductor, a worm, a bat, an alien, a robot, and a bulldog), six worlds with their own girder materials, a live jazz soundtrack that reacts to your combo, and a 1930s "rubber hose" cartoon look with film effects.

"Cups" shown in the Characters screen are trophies earned by playing; they cannot be bought.

The app is designed for iPhone (portrait). On iPad it runs in iPhone compatibility mode; all screens were tested there.
The game is available in 14 languages and follows the device language (per-app language is supported).
Privacy policy and support links are in Settings > About.
