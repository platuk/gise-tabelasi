**Description (4000):**
CupTower is an endless climbing game inspired by 1930s rubber hose cartoons.

Play with one finger: hold the left half of the screen to run left and the right half to run right; your character jumps by itself every time it lands. The faster you run, the higher you jump. Flip off the walls, chain multi-floor jumps into combos, and stay ahead of the burning film rising from below!

• A trophy every 100 floors: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, Legend. The trophy floor is your checkpoint.
• A boss every 1000 floors: an octopus conductor, a professor worm, a count bat, an alien in a flying saucer, a rusty robot, and a bulldog hanging from a crane hook. Each has its own attacks, and every attack is announced in advance.
• Six worlds: harbor, garden, haunted castle, space, factory, and city, with new girder materials in every chapter.
• Surprise bubbles: rocket, slow motion, adrenaline, shield.
• Five characters, unlocked with the trophies you win (no real-money purchases).
• Boss fights mode: fight only the bosses and beat all six in a row.
• Jazz music and sound effects generated in code; the band grows as your combo grows.
• Game speed: Normal, Medium, Fast.
• No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet needed. No data is collected.
